Verify Graphify root/ESM exports, alias declarations, refresh bundle, profile/registry files, poppler and lock availability. No host Node/Python and no provider calls in CI.

The shared storage must support cross-pod POSIX locks; a fresh pod must fail/exit retryably while the previous refresh holds the lock, and succeed after release. Promote only after scheduled execution, pod replacement + real durable credential refresh, and recovery/idempotence evidence.
