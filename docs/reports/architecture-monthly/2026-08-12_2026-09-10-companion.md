## 5. Remaining LLM allocation question

**Which auditable method should allocate LLM expense to Immo/Geo for this exact
reporting window?** Focus offers three neutral, unselected drafts:

| Method | Basis | Main strength | Main limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DIRECT | Only supplier expenses unambiguously attributable to the products | Closest to observed incurred expense | Seat subscriptions may have no product-level line |
| USAGE | Deduplicated calls in the window, weighted by provider and token type | Reflects period product usage | Requires exact input/cache-write/cache-read/output attribution |
| CAPACITY | Historical seven-day peak projected to thirty days | Preserves the earlier calculation's continuity | It allocates capacity and is not a provider invoice |

The previously reported combined LLM value **214.743159 CAD** is the output of
the historical-peak capacity allocation. It is not a supplier invoice or a final
bill. The monetary proposal remains **audit incomplete**, with no default method.

Required closure evidence: exact local-time inclusion, deduplication across
resumed/copied sessions and repeated messages/calls, per-provider/model breakdown
of input/cache-write/cache-read/output, actual API/subscription expense, shared
cost rule, margin rule and a reproducible calculation for every proposed amount.
